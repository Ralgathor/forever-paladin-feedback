Proposed changes

These are the changes that we propose for the Forever Paladin. They follow the design rules of Forever: no talents that improve a buff, talents in rows 1–2 that the other specs want, and a 1-point talent at 30 points. Push Proposed above to make a build with them. Select a row in a talent table to go to that talent.
