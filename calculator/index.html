Proposed changes

This section lists the proposed changes for the Paladin in WoW Forever. They follow the design rules of Forever: no talents that improve a buff, talents in Rows 1 and 2 that the other specs want, and a 1-point talent at 30 points. Push Proposed above to see the new trees. Select a talent name to go to that talent.

The Paladin compared, by role

A card with Live and Proposed values shows the effect of a proposal. A card with the Live only tag compares the live Paladin with the other classes. The values come from the models and from wowsims, not from combat logs. Level 60, one level 63 boss, unless a card gives a different condition. The proposals document gives the details and the limits of each value.

Click a talent to add a point, right-click to remove one. Shift-click fills or empties a talent. On a phone, tap a talent and use − and + in the card below the tree. Each row opens after 5 points per row in that tree, and a talent with an arrow needs the talent above it at full rank.

In Live mode, tiers, rank counts, prerequisites and tooltips follow the Forever live data (3 November 2026, same trees as the beta of 30 October): client build 1.60.1.70755 with hotfix push 113578, from the game files in wowsims Forever. Blizzard has not published patch notes for the trees. Build 70755 adds Holy damage to Two-Handed Weapon Specialization, 5 stacks to Vengeance and a level 60 rank of Holy Shield (221 damage, 4 charges). Holy Shield shows rank 1 (75 damage). Live build codes start with b2. A code with only digits is a code for the beta trees of 18 October 2026. It opens on the page of the 18 October beta. The proposed trees use the live trees as their base. The grid layout matches Wowhead's Forever calculator. The talent icons load from the Wowhead image server when you open this file in a browser. Versions. v6, 31 October 2026: the proposed trees use the live trees. The live version adopts six proposals and makes twenty others unnecessary. The kept proposals use free places in the new rows. Toughness is removed (C-P13), and Devoted Sacrifice takes its place. 2 November 2026: Reach of Law (C-P7) is new in Protection row 5, column 1, and Stoicism moves to row 4, column 4 (M-P2). Swift Judgement no longer requires Templar's Bulwark (C-P14). Reverence has 25/50% (C-H1). 3 November 2026: Light's Vigil becomes a buff on Holy Shock (C-H7). Oath of Light gives 30% (C-H8). Righteous Fury increases all threat by 90% (C25). It keeps its place. The proposed trees do not show withdrawn talents. New talents go at the end of the tree code. Thus, build codes do not change. Build codes start with v6p. v5, 29 October 2026: two new Holy talents in rows 5 and 4, two new Retribution talents in row 5, and Divine Precision goes to Holy row 4 (M-H5). The new talents go at the end of each tree code, thus version 5 codes stay valid. v5, 26 October 2026: Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11) is new in Protection row 3. Shoulder the Burden goes to row 3, column 1, and Reach of Law to row 5. Swift Judgement and Improved Hammer of Justice are in their live places again. Build codes start with v5p. v4, 24 October 2026: Precision, Guardian's Favor, Anticipation and Holy Conduit are in their live places again. Benediction (5 ranks, 2–10%) goes to Protection row 1. Vindication goes to Retribution row 2, column 3. Build codes start with v4p. v3, 24 October 2026: Consecrated Ground is in Holy row 5 again, as in the live trees. Build codes start with v3p. v2, 23 October 2026: Toughness and Divine Strength are removed. Precision and Guardian's Favor go to Protection row 1, Holy Conduit to Protection row 2, Vindication (5 ranks) to Retribution row 2 and Unyielding Faith to Holy row 1. Improved Seals keeps 3 ranks. Build codes start with v2p. v1, before 23 October 2026: the earlier proposed trees. Build codes start with p.
