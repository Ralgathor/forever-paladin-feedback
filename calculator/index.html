Proposed changes

This section lists the proposed changes for the Paladin in WoW Forever. They follow the design rules of Forever: no talents that improve a buff, talents in Rows 1 and 2 that the other specs want, and a 1-point talent at 30 points. Push Proposed above to see the new trees. Select a talent name to go to that talent.

Versions. v6, 31 October 2026: the proposed trees use the beta trees of 30 October 2026. The beta adopts six proposals and makes twenty others unnecessary. The kept proposals use free places in the new rows. Build codes start with v6p. v5, 29 October 2026: Merciful Light (C-H5) and Divine Grace (C-H6) are new in Holy rows 5 and 4, and Divine Precision goes to Holy row 4 (M-H5). Burning Zeal (C-R4) and Measure for Measure (C-R5) are new in Retribution row 5. The new talents go at the end of each tree code, thus version 5 codes stay valid. v5, 26 October 2026: Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11) is new in Protection row 3. Shoulder the Burden goes to row 3, column 1, and Reach of Law to row 5. Swift Judgement and Improved Hammer of Justice are in their beta places again. Build codes start with v5p. v4, 24 October 2026: Precision, Guardian's Favor, Anticipation and Holy Conduit are in their beta places again. Benediction (5 ranks, 2–10%) goes to Protection row 1. Vindication goes to Retribution row 2, column 3. Build codes start with v4p. v3, 24 October 2026: Consecrated Ground is in Holy row 5 again, as in the beta. Build codes start with v3p. v2, 23 October 2026: Toughness and Divine Strength removal (C-P3) deletes both talents. Precision and Guardian's Favor go to Protection row 1, Holy Conduit to Protection row 2, Vindication (5 ranks) to Retribution row 2 and Unyielding Faith to Holy row 1. Improved Seals keeps 3 ranks. Build codes start with v2p. v1, before 23 October 2026: the earlier proposed trees. Build codes start with p.
