# wowsims check on the beta of 30 October

These files add the proposals to wowsims Forever, master branch (commit `6ee3c30f35`, 4 November 2026, game files of client 1.60.1.70755). The Paladin and Warrior values are the same as on commit `d97252f2a` (3 November 2026). The Druid, Fury, Rogue and Hunter values changed with the hotfix 113578 ports of 4 November. They run the default Retribution set: default gear, rotation and consumables (with Major Mana Potion), build 14/6/31.

On 4 November 2026 (later), all checks ran again on master commit `8298ab833` (`logs/checks_8298ab833.log`). The Paladin, Druid and Retribution values do not change. The Protection Warrior has new talents and a new rotation: +6.4% threat on one boss (907 → 965 per sec, before the ×1.445 of the notes). The Charge opener APL (`apl/charge.apl.json`) does not work on this commit. On commit 8298ab833, `patches/talents.patch` needs `patch -p1 --fuzz=3`: the commit adds a menu group line in `applyReckoning`. The runs of 1 November used commit `895cd67f9`, and the earlier runs used branch `paladin/hotfix-113554-port` (commit `db28d84a8`) with the build 14/5/32. Commit `d97252f2a` has the game data of build 70755: Holy Shield rank 4 (221 damage, 4 charges) and the two Retribution changes of `notes-2-november.patch`. Thus, do not use the `N2H` and `V5` flags on this commit. The `C1` flag panics in the Protection test on this commit (Seal of Fury).

On the evening of 4 November 2026, the tank and Protection runs ran again on master commit `9a8624af9` (`logs/checks_9a8624af9_pool.log`, `logs/checks_9a8624af9_pool_surv.log`, `logs/finals_9a8624af9.log`, `logs/finals_surv_9a8624af9.log`). This commit reads Defensive Stance +80%, Defiance 20% and Bear Form +115% from the hotfixes of 4 November, and gives Maul and Swipe ×1.75 and Primal Bite ×3. Thus the Warrior and Druid values are native: no ×1.445 or ×1.433. The Paladin test now has tank consumables (`feedbackConsumables` in `go/feedback_prot_test.go`; `FEEDBACK_CONSUMES=default` gives the Demonic Rune only). `patches/talents.patch` does not apply on this commit: use `patches/talents-9a8624af9.patch`. The Retribution values do not change.

Control run on commit `9a8624af9` (item pool threat set, `FEEDBACK_CONSUMES=default`): live 1,029.3 threat each sec / 383.8 damage taken, `CP1` 1,107.3 / 339.3, `C25` 1,463.1. These are the values of commit 8298ab833. Thus, the Paladin spells and the proposal flags do not change, and the new values come from the consumables only. The patches change only Paladin files and the threat recorder of the core (`burst-threat.patch`), not the Warrior or Druid files. `notes-2-november.patch` is not applied on this commit. The native Warrior value (1,447.7) is the old value × 1.445 (1,002.1 × 1.445 = 1,448.0). The Druid gets more (1,755 against 779.0 × 1.433 = 1,116), because Maul, Swipe and Primal Bite now have their threat multipliers in wowsims.

On 6 November 2026, master commit `df57e2571` (hotfix push 113693, client 1.60.1.70790) adds 2,447 items. The tank searches ran again on this commit (`tank_gear/README.md`). Only the Warrior threat set changes (Scarlet Kris in place of Greatflank Flayer). The Warrior jobs ran again with the new set (`logs/checks_df57e2571_pool.log`): 1,503.0 threat each sec (was 1,447.7), 305.8 damage taken, TMI 43, 1.1% crushing blows. The Paladin, Druid and Shockadin sets give the same values on this commit. Thus, the other logs stay.

On 11 November 2026, master commit `3633d376a` (client 1.60.1.70888, hotfixes of 10 and 11 November) adds 464 items, for example Ironfoe. This commit needs Go 1.27 (`GOTOOLCHAIN=go1.27.3`), and the protos change: generate them again as in `run.sh`. `patches/talents-9a8624af9.patch` and `patches/burst-threat.patch` apply on this commit. The Warrior has 5.3% more threat (1,511.8 → 1,591.8): Shield Slam ranks 2 to 4 have flat threat. The Retribution and Shockadin values do not change. First, the sets of 9 November ran on this commit: `logs/checks_3633d376a_pool_old_sets.log` and `logs/checks_3633d376a_pool_surv_old_sets.log`. All checks and builds ran again with the new sets: `logs/checks_3633d376a_pool.log`, `logs/checks_3633d376a_pool_surv.log`, `logs/finals_3633d376a.log`, `logs/finals_surv_3633d376a.log` and `logs/finals_shock_3633d376a.log`. On 13 November 2026, master commit `8c7b5a5f5` (client 1.60.1.70938, hotfixes of 13 November) adds 46 items. Two examples are The Lion Horn of Stormwind and Gauntlets of Heroism. It changes Redoubt and Shieldsworn: each melee swing or ability against the Paladin rolls the 10% Redoubt chance, avoided attacks included. It also fixes Two-Handed Weapon Specialization for Retribution. The hotfixes of 11 November make Judgement a ranged attack, but wowsims still makes the damage judgements melee attacks. Thus, `patches/judgement-ranged.patch` makes them ranged attacks: they use the ranged hit chance and no longer trigger melee weapon procs. The late hotfix of 13 November makes Judgement of Command a melee attack again. Judgement of Righteousness and Judgement of Fury stay ranged. The patch now changes only these two. All runs of this round use this patch (`JMELEE` gives the old melee judgements). All Retribution and Shockadin runs use the new patch. Protection uses Judgement of Fury and does not change. `patches/talents-9a8624af9.patch` and `patches/burst-threat.patch` apply on this commit. First, the sets of 12 November, evening ran on this commit: `logs/checks_8c7b5a5f5_pool_old_sets.log` and `logs/checks_8c7b5a5f5_pool_surv_old_sets.log`. The live Paladin gains 0.7% threat (1,133.4 → 1,141.1) and takes 2.4% less damage (336.1 → 328.2). C-P1 loses 0.5% threat (1,208.2 → 1,201.8). Retribution loses 1.5% (837.6 → 825.0) with all three judgements ranged: −1.2% from the ranged judgements, −0.3% from the Two-Handed Weapon Specialization fix. With Judgement of Command melee, it has 830.6. The Warrior and the Druid do not change. The damage, tank and Shockadin searches then ran again (`matched_gear/README.md`, `tank_gear/README.md`). All checks and builds ran again with the new sets: `logs/checks_8c7b5a5f5_pool.log`, `logs/checks_8c7b5a5f5_pool_surv.log`, `logs/checks_8c7b5a5f5_warrior_dps.log`, `logs/finals.log`, `logs/finals_surv.log` and `logs/finals_shock.log`. The logs of 12 November, evening (129fcd89e) have the commit in the file name (`finals_129fcd89e.log` and the others).

| File | What it does |

| go/proposals_sim.go | Flags in `PROPOSE`: `C1` (Holy Strike triggers your active Seal, withdrawn on 2 November 2026), `BZ` (Burning Zeal 2/2, withdrawn), `UW20` (Unsealed Wrath: Seal cost −20%), `CP1` (each Holy Shield cast starts Redoubt), `RD20` (Redoubt 4–20% in place of 6–30%), `CP6` (Reckoning C-P6: charges from blocks, dodges and parries, Holy Strike releases 2), `CP6R` (beta Reckoning triggers, Holy Strike releases 2), `RKB` (the beta block chance and limit also on dodges and parries), `CP6A` (C-P6 triggers with the beta release on the next auto attack), `RK100` (Reckoning block chance 100%), `RKICD4` (Reckoning block limit 4 sec), `NOTOUGH` (Toughness removal, C-P13: Toughness gives no armor), `REV50` (Reverence 25/50%, C-H1), `CH7` (Light's Vigil, C-H7, on an enemy: Holy Shock, Judgement and Holy Strike give a stack, once each 6 sec, up to 3; a Holy Shock with a stack incurs no cooldown), `SA10` and `SA0` (Sacred Arbiter +10% or +0% in place of +20%), `C25` (Righteous Fury: all threat +90% in place of +33%; `RF_ALL` sets the multiplier, default 1.90), `JMELEE` (melee judgements as in wowsims master, with `patches/judgement-ranged.patch`) |

| go/feedback_bear_test.go | The same for the Druid tank (default Bear set): threat, damage taken, TMI and crushing blows |

| `FEEDBACK_DUMP` | In the three tank tests and the Retribution test: a file path. The test writes its sim request to this file as JSON. sets/zzsets_main.go makes the wowsims links from these files |

The enchants of each slot stay, and an item of the default set keeps its random suffix. tank_gear/run_pool.sh, run_seal_plans.sh and search.py use it: search.py and finals.py run the item pool sets (`FEEDBACK_GEAR=default` keeps the default sets) |
| go/allspecs_main.go, logs/allspecs.log | All damage specs with pre-raid gear and the same buffs (Analysis, Damage, part 7). The file header gives the steps |
| matched_gear/ | The pure damage specs and Retribution with the same pre-raid item pool: the pure average of Analysis, Damage. See matched_gear/README.md |
| sets/ | The wowsims links of the tank sets (item pool and default) and the builds sets. See sets/README.md |

| tank_gear/ | The item pool tank sets of Analysis, Threat (no raid, PvP, Zul'Gurub, Ahn'Qiraj or Revered and Exalted reputation items, item level 63 or lower), the search and the runs. See tank_gear/README.md |
| patches/notes-2-november.patch | Flags for the development notes of 2 November 2026: `N2H` (Two-Handed Weapon Specialization also increases Holy damage by 2/4/6%) and `V5` (Vengeance stacks up to 5 times).
