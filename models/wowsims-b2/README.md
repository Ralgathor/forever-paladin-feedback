# wowsims check on the beta of 30 October

These files add the proposals to wowsims Forever, master branch (commit `6ee3c30f35`, 4 November 2026, game files of client 1.60.1.70755). The Paladin and Warrior values are the same as on commit `d97252f2a` (3 November 2026). The Druid, Fury, Rogue and Hunter values changed with the hotfix 113578 ports of 4 November. They run the default Retribution set: default gear, rotation and consumables (with Major Mana Potion), build 14/6/31.

On 4 November 2026 (later), all checks ran again on master commit `8298ab833` (`logs/checks_8298ab833.log`). The Paladin, Druid and Retribution values do not change. The Protection Warrior has new talents and a new rotation: +6.4% threat on one boss (907 → 965 per sec, before the ×1.445 of the notes). The Charge opener APL (`apl/charge.apl.json`) does not work on this commit. On commit 8298ab833, `patches/talents.patch` needs `patch -p1 --fuzz=3`: the commit adds a menu group line in `applyReckoning`. The runs of 1 November used commit `895cd67f9`, and the earlier runs used branch `paladin/hotfix-113554-port` (commit `db28d84a8`) with the build 14/5/32. Commit `d97252f2a` has the game data of build 70755: Holy Shield rank 4 (221 damage, 4 charges) and the two Retribution changes of `notes-2-november.patch`. Thus, do not use the `N2H` and `V5` flags on this commit. The `C1` flag panics in the Protection test on this commit (Seal of Fury).

On the evening of 4 November 2026, the tank and Protection runs ran again on master commit `9a8624af9` (`logs/checks_9a8624af9_pool.log`, `logs/checks_9a8624af9_pool_surv.log`, `logs/finals.log`, `logs/finals_surv.log`). This commit reads Defensive Stance +80%, Defiance 20% and Bear Form +115% from the hotfixes of 4 November, and gives Maul and Swipe ×1.75 and Primal Bite ×3. Thus the Warrior and Druid values are native: no ×1.445 or ×1.433. The Paladin test now has tank consumables (`feedbackConsumables` in `go/feedback_prot_test.go`; `FEEDBACK_CONSUMES=default` gives the Demonic Rune only). `patches/talents.patch` does not apply on this commit: use `patches/talents-9a8624af9.patch`. The Retribution values do not change.

Control run on commit `9a8624af9` (item pool threat set, `FEEDBACK_CONSUMES=default`): live 1,029.3 threat each sec / 383.8 damage taken, `CP1` 1,107.3 / 339.3, `C25` 1,463.1. These are the values of commit 8298ab833. Thus, the Paladin spells and the proposal flags do not change, and the new values come from the consumables only. The patches change only Paladin files and the threat recorder of the core (`burst-threat.patch`), not the Warrior or Druid files. `notes-2-november.patch` is not applied on this commit. The native Warrior value (1,447.7) is the old value × 1.445 (1,002.1 × 1.445 = 1,448.0). The Druid gets more (1,755 against 779.0 × 1.433 = 1,116), because Maul, Swipe and Primal Bite now have their threat multipliers in wowsims.

The folders: `patches/` (the wowsims patches), `go/` (the sim flags and the test files), `apl/` (the rotations), `logs/` (the run logs). The logs of commit 6ee3c30f35 are `checks_6ee3c30f35.log` and `finals.log` (the same values as on commit d97252f2a). `checks_d97252f2a.log` has the runs of 3 November. `finals_895cd67f9.log` has the runs of commit 895cd67f9. The scripts stay in this folder.

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| go/proposals_sim.go | Flags in `PROPOSE`: `C1` (Holy Strike triggers your active Seal, withdrawn on 2 November 2026), `BZ` (Burning Zeal 2/2, withdrawn), `UW20` (Unsealed Wrath: Seal cost −20%), `CP1` (each Holy Shield cast starts Redoubt), `RD20` (Redoubt 4–20% in place of 6–30%), `CP6` (Reckoning C-P6: charges from blocks, dodges and parries, Holy Strike releases 2), `CP6R` (beta Reckoning triggers, Holy Strike releases 2), `RKB` (the beta block chance and limit also on dodges and parries), `CP6A` (C-P6 triggers with the beta release on the next auto attack), `RK100` (Reckoning block chance 100%), `RKICD4` (Reckoning block limit 4 sec), `NOTOUGH` (Toughness removal, C-P13: Toughness gives no armor), `REV50` (Reverence 25/50%, C-H1), `CH7` (Light's Vigil, C-H7, on an enemy: Holy Shock, Judgement and Holy Strike give a stack, once each 6 sec, up to 3; a Holy Shock with a stack incurs no cooldown), `SA10` and `SA0` (Sacred Arbiter +10% or +0% in place of +20%), `C25` (Righteous Fury: all threat +90% in place of +33%; `RF_ALL` sets the multiplier, default 1.90) |
| patches/talents.patch | Calls the flags after the talents, the Reckoning flags and the `NOTOUGH` flag |
| go/feedback_run_test.go | One Retribution sim: `FEEDBACK_DUR` (sec), `FEEDBACK_BUFFS` (`full`, `lean` or `none`), 3,000 iterations. `FEEDBACK_SHOCK=1`: the wowsims Holy Shock set, item swap set, rotation, talents and consumables (builds/shockadin.md). `FEEDBACK_BOOKTOTEMS=1`: Strength of Earth Totem and Grace of Air Totem at the book rank of the Ruins of Ahn'Qiraj (rank 2 in the raid buffs) |
| go/feedback_prot_test.go | One Protection sim (default tank set, full buffs, level 63 boss in front): threat, damage taken, TMI (6 sec window) and the boss attack table |
| go/feedback_warrior_test.go | The same for the Protection Warrior (pre-raid set), for comparison |
| go/feedback_bear_test.go | The same for the Druid tank (default Bear set): threat, damage taken, TMI and crushing blows |
| go/feedback_warrior_dps_test.go | One Fury or Arms Warrior sim for Analysis, Damage, part 9: `FEEDBACK_SPEC` (`fury` or `arms`), `FEEDBACK_GEAR` (`prebis` or `p1`), full buffs |
| `FEEDBACK_MANA` | In the Paladin tank test: also print the mana sources (Analysis, Mana) |
| `FEEDBACK_BOSS` | In the Paladin and Warrior tank tests: `fast` (a swing each 1.2 sec, 60% damage), `dw` (two weapons), or both |
| `FEEDBACK_TARGETS` | In the three tank tests: the number of targets. Each target hits for 1/3 of the boss. The tests then print the threat on each target |
| `FEEDBACK_DUMP` | In the three tank tests and the Retribution test: a file path. The test writes its sim request to this file as JSON. sets/zzsets_main.go makes the wowsims links from these files |
| `FEEDBACK_ITEMS` | In the three tank tests and the Retribution test (also with `FEEDBACK_SHOCK=1`): 17 item ids, separated by commas (0 = empty slot). They replace the items of the default set. The enchants of each slot stay, and an item of the default set keeps its random suffix. tank_gear/run_pool.sh, run_seal_plans.sh and search.py use it: search.py and finals.py run the item pool sets (`FEEDBACK_GEAR=default` keeps the default sets) |
| go/allspecs_main.go, logs/allspecs.log | All damage specs with pre-raid gear and the same buffs (Analysis, Damage, part 7). The file header gives the steps |
| matched_gear/ | The pure damage specs and Retribution with the same pre-raid item pool: the pure average of Analysis, Damage. See matched_gear/README.md |
| sets/ | The wowsims links of the tank sets (item pool and default) and the builds sets. See sets/README.md |
| seal_plans_apl.py, run_seal_plans.sh | Seal plans and Seal pairs of Retribution (W29): APLs made from the default APL, and the runs on the item pool Retribution set (`logs/seal_plans_8298ab833_pool.log`) |
| tank_gear/ | The item pool tank sets of Analysis, Threat (no raid, PvP, Zul'Gurub, Ahn'Qiraj or Revered and Exalted reputation items, item level 63 or lower), the search and the runs. See tank_gear/README.md |
| patches/notes-2-november.patch | Flags for the development notes of 2 November 2026: `N2H` (Two-Handed Weapon Specialization also increases Holy damage by 2/4/6%) and `V5` (Vengeance stacks up to 5 times). Apply after talents.patch. Result on commit 895cd67f9 (default Retribution set, full buffs, 2 min): live 905.9 DPS, `N2H` 938.7, `V5` 946.3, both 980.8. Commit d97252f2a without flags: 937.9 |
| patches/threat-options.patch | Threat options for the gap after the notes of 2 November 2026 (Analysis, Threat), off by default. `PROPOSE=CP9`: Holy Shield adds block value × `CP9_BV` (default 2.0) to each hit (C-P9, withdrawn on 31 October). `HS_THREAT`: the Holy Shield threat multiplier (live 1.2). `CP6_ICD`: the C-P6 limit on blocks, dodges and parries in sec (default 4). Apply after talents.patch. Result on commit 895cd67f9 (3 min, with `CP1`, Warrior 1,338 TPS after the notes): `CP1` 986, `CP9` at 1.0 1,089, at 2.0 1,192; `HS_THREAT` 2.0 1,120, 2.25 1,162, 2.5 1,203; `CP6A` with `CP6_ICD` 4 1,029, 2 1,108, 1 1,137; `HS_THREAT=2.0` with `CP9` at 1.0 1,292 |
| patches/burst-threat.patch | Analysis, Threat, part 8. A threat recorder in the core: with `FEEDBACK_BURST=<label>`, the three tank tests print the threat on the boss after 1.5, 3, 4.5, 6, 8, 10, 15, 20 and 30 sec (mean, 10% and 90% of the fights) and the best 3, 6 and 10 sec windows of the fight. The fight length is then fixed. Apply after threat-options.patch |
| apl/furyopen.apl.json, apl/furyopen1.apl.json | Paladin Fury openers for part 8 (`FEEDBACK_APL=furyopen`): Seal of Fury at −3 sec and Holy Shield at −1.5 sec, then Judgement of Fury, Swift Judgement and Holy Strike at 0 sec. `furyopen1`: Swift Judgement at 1.5 sec.
