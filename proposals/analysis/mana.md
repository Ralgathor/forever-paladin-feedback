# Mana

This section compares the mana of the Paladin in the beta and with the proposals. The unit is mana per second (MPS). A positive value means that the mana increases. A negative value means that the mana decreases.

## Summary

- Sanctified Judgement (Retribution row 3) is the best mana talent for a tank. It returns 120 mana for each Judgement: 14 MPS, or 17 MPS with Improved Judgement 2/2. Reverence gives 10.3 MPS.
- The tank builds PB1–PB4 (3/35/13, see Builds) have Sanctified Judgement, Improved Judgement and Holy Conduit. With Consecration on cooldown, the mana lasts 1 min 25 sec at fresh level 60 and 9 min 56 sec in Naxxramas. With Blessing of Wisdom, the Naxxramas tank almost does not lose mana.
- Reverence (C1) stays the main mana talent for Holy. Retribution also takes it.
- An 8/35/8 tank with Reverence at fresh level 60 has almost no extra mana while it tanks: +3.9 MPS with the proposals, +1.5 MPS in the beta.
- The tank gets more mana with better gear. In Naxxramas, the tank gets +29.8 MPS with the proposals.
- C13 (Shield Specialization once each 2 sec) is necessary. Without C13, a fresh level 60 tank loses about 9 MPS.
- Consecration uses much mana. Use it against groups of enemies, not all the time.
- M11 puts Benediction in Protection row 1. With Consecration on cooldown, the mana then lasts 52 sec at fresh level 60 and 2 min 45 sec in Naxxramas. With Holy Conduit also (7 Retribution points), it lasts 1 min 36 sec and 19 min (part 4).
- C17 (Twist of Light) fixes the mana of Retribution.

## 1. Problems in the beta

- **Spirit regeneration stops when you cast.** It gives 15 + Spirit ÷ 5 mana each 2 sec. It stops for 5 sec after each cast. In combat, it is almost zero.
- **Protection and Retribution have no talent for regeneration while casting.** Other classes give 50% to their damage and hybrid specs at about 20 points (see What the other classes have).
- **Protection receives mana mainly when it is hit.** Shield Specialization and Improved Seal of Fury give this mana. The other source is Sanctified Judgement in Retribution row 3. A tank needs 13 Retribution points for it.
- **Retribution loses mana because of Seal swaps.** The cause is not the regeneration.

## 2. Fixes

| Fix | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Reverence in row 2 at 50% (C1) | Holy has 50%, as the other healers. Retribution takes it with 8 Holy points: about 10 MPS. Enhancement Shaman takes Mindfulness in the same way. For a tank, Sanctified Judgement gives more (part 4) |
| Shield Specialization once each 2 sec (C13) | It stays the main mana source of the tank. Water Shield (Shaman) is the Forever precedent |
| Improved Seal of Fury removed (C10) | One talent for "mana when you are hit" is sufficient |
| Echo of 10 sec (C17) | Retribution changes Seals one time each 10 sec, not before each swing |

## 3. Inputs

**Gear**

- Maximum mana and mp5 come from the gear tiers (see Threat, part 2).
- Fresh level 60 uses the real gear profile: 2,897 mana, 3 mp5.
- Tank gear does not give much Spirit. All tiers use 130 Spirit.

**Combat**

- The boss swings each 2.0 sec.
- One-handed weapon: 2.7 sec.
- Block: 50% in the beta, 60% with C6.

**Spell costs at level 60 (Forever spellbook)**

| Spell | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Holy Shield rank 3 | 240 mana, each 10 sec |
| Holy Strike | 20 mana, each 10 sec |
| Judgement | 91 mana (6% of base mana 1,512), each 10 sec |
| Seal of Fury rank 7 | 200 mana, each 30 sec |
| Seal of Command | 210 mana |
| Consecration rank 5 | 565 mana, each 8 sec |

- Base mana 1,512 is the Classic value. It is not confirmed for Forever.
- Holy Conduit (Consecration ×0.60) is in Retribution row 2, as in the beta. A tank takes it with Deflection 5 (7 points).
- Benediction 5/5 (all costs ×0.90) is in Protection row 1 (M11). In the beta, it is in Retribution row 1. The beta columns use the same talents from a Retribution dip.
- Sanctified Judgement 3/3 returns 60% of the base cost of the Seal for each Judgement. Seal of Fury rank 7 costs 200: the return is 120 mana. The free Judgement of Swift Judgement also gives the return (wowsims Forever).
- Improved Judgement 2/2 (Retribution row 2) decreases the Judgement cooldown to 8 sec. The tank then uses 8.5 Judgements each min, not 7.
- Raid buffs are not in the model. Blessing of Wisdom gives 8 MPS. Judgement of Wisdom on the boss gives more.

## 6. What the results mean

- **The tank gets more mana with gear.** Shield Specialization returns 4% of maximum mana for each block. More mana gives a larger return.
- **At fresh level 60, the tank has almost no extra mana.** It gets +3.9 MPS with the proposals. Without C13, it loses about 9 MPS.
- **In raids, the tank does not lose mana without Consecration.** It gets +10 to +30 MPS (beta: +5 to +17).
- **Consecration on cooldown uses all mana quickly:** in 43 sec at fresh level 60, in about 2 min in Naxxramas. With Benediction in Protection (M11), it lasts 52 sec to 2 min 45 sec. With Holy Conduit from a 7-point Retribution dip also, it lasts 1 min 36 sec to 19 min.
- **Holy Conduit gives the large decrease with Consecration.** With Benediction, it saves about 25 mana per sec (W4).
- **Sanctified Judgement is the best mana talent for a tank.** It gives 14–17 MPS, also when the boss does not hit the tank. Reverence gives 10.3 MPS. It stays the mana talent for Holy and Retribution.
- **C26 does not change the total return.** It changes only ranks 1 and 2. Each point now gives the same mana.
- **The 3/35/13 tank can use Consecration on cooldown for a long time.** With Holy Conduit, its mana lasts 1 min 25 sec at fresh level 60 and almost 10 min in Naxxramas.
- **Flatter gear gives less mana.** In late raids, the tank gets +14 to +20 MPS. C13 is then more important.
- **Intellect is a tank stat for the Paladin.** It gives more mana. It also increases the Shield Specialization return.
- **Retribution with C17 almost does not lose mana** when it has Reverence.
