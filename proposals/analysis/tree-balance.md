# Tree balance

This section uses the proposed trees of calculator version 6: the beta trees of 30 October 2026 with the proposals. The builds come from Analysis, Builds.

Three rules:

1. Rows 1 and 2 must give something to the other specs.
2. Row 3 is an investment choice. Another spec puts 10 points or more in the tree for it.
3. Talents must be in parity with the talents of other classes, not equal. A Paladin talent can be stronger when this is a strength of the class.

The comparison uses the 24 trees of the other classes (wowsims Forever talent data, 25 October 2026).

## How the other classes use rows 1–3

| Row | Usual content | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A main stat, or +5% critical hit or hit, for all specs of the class | Cruelty, Malice, Lethal Attacks, Thundering Strikes (+5% critical hit). Elemental Precision, Shadow Focus, Suppression (+5% hit). Ancestral Knowledge, Heart of the Wild (+10% stat). Deflection (parry) |
| 2 | Efficiency, utility, more stats. Sometimes the healer mana talent | Precision (Rogue), Mental Strength (Priest), Mindfulness (Shaman), Nature's Majesty (+4% critical hit), Elusiveness (Rogue) |
| 3 | A 1-point signature talent, and investment talents | Anger Management, Last Stand, Riposte, Pyroblast, Inner Focus, Holy Nova, Water Shield, Feral Charge. Healer mana: Meditation, Reflection |

## Rule 1: rows 1 and 2 give something to the other specs

| Tree | Rows 1–2 | Retribution receives | Protection receives | Holy receives |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Holy | Spiritual Focus, Divine Strength · Healing Light, Reverence, Improved Seals, Purifying Power | +10% Strength, regeneration during casts, +15% Seals and Judgements (RB1: 14 points with Holy Conduit) | Nothing that the tank takes | — |
| Protection | Divine Intellect, Redoubt · Precision, Guardian's Favor, Sacred Duty | +10% Intellect (RB1: 5 points). PvP: Guardian's Favor, Sacred Duty | — | +10% Intellect, Hand of Protection each 3 min, Divine Shield −60 sec (HB2: 12 points with Devoted Sacrifice) |
| Retribution | Deflection, Benediction · Improved Judgement, Conviction, Sanctified Judgement | — | Parry, instant spells −10%, Judgement −2 sec, +6% critical hit, Judgement mana (PT1: 17 points) | Instant spells −10%, +6% critical heal chance (HB1, HB2: 8 points) |

- **Each tree gives something to two specs, except Holy for the tank.** The tank has no use for Holy rows 1–2. The tank already has the most points outside its tree (17), in Retribution.
- **Holy healers and the Protection dip.** Divine Intellect 5 opens row 2. Guardian's Favor and Sacred Duty open row 3 for Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11). Thus, the dip is all saves and mana, with no filler.

## Rule 2: row 3 is an investment

| Tree | Row 3 | Who puts 10 points or more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Holy | Divine Favor (1-point signature), Illumination, Holy Conduit | Retribution: Holy Conduit (RB1, 14 Holy points) |
| Protection | Templar's Bulwark (1-point signature), Improved Hammer of Justice, Shield Specialization, Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11) | Holy healers: Devoted Sacrifice (12 points). PvP builds: Hammer of Justice each 45 sec and the way to Swift Judgement (RB2, RB3: 20–25 points) |
| Retribution | Seal of Command (1-point signature), Vindication, Unyielding Faith, Pursuit of Justice | None. The tank and Holy stop in row 2 |

- **Devoted Sacrifice in row 3 (Toughness removal, C-P13)** gives Protection row 3 an investment for Holy. In row 4, it cost 17 points, and Holy did not take it.
- **Retribution row 3** has no investment for the other specs. Unyielding Faith (fear) and Pursuit of Justice (run speed) are PvP talents. A tank or a healer does not need them in raids.

## Rule 3: parity with other classes

| Paladin talent | Comparable talents | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Divine Intellect (+10% Intellect, Protection row 1) | Ancestral Knowledge (row 1), Mental Strength (row 2) | Equal |
| Deflection (+5% parry, Retribution row 1) | Deflection (Warrior, Hunter, row 1) | Equal |
| Benediction (instant spells −10%, Retribution row 1) | Convection (−10%, 5 points, row 1) | Equal |
| Conviction (+6% critical hit, 3 points, Retribution row 2) | Cruelty, Malice, Lethal Attacks (+5%, 5 points, row 1) | Stronger for each point, one row deeper |
| Precision (+3% hit, Protection row 2) | Precision (Rogue, row 2), Elemental Precision (row 1) | Equal |
| Reverence (15/30%, Holy row 2) | Mindfulness (row 2), Meditation and Reflection (row 3) | Equal |
| Improved Seals (+15% Seals and Judgements, 5 points, Holy row 2) | Concussion (+5%), Improved Eviscerate (+20%) | Parity |
| Improved Judgement (−2 sec, 2 points) | Improved Mind Blast (−2.5 sec, 5 points) | Stronger for each point. It is the Classic value |
| Anticipation (+20 Defense, Protection row 4) | Anticipation (Warrior, row 2) | Weaker: two rows deeper |
| Two-Handed Weapon Specialization (+6% physical, Retribution row 4) | Two-Handed Weapon Specialization (Warrior, +3%, row 4) | Stronger, intentional. Retribution is the two-handed spec of the class. In the beta of 30 October, it no longer increases Seals and Judgements |
| Toughness (removed, C-P13) | No armor talent for the Protection Warrior. Thick Hide (Druid) | The Paladin tank with Redoubt (C-P1) does not need it (see Protection, C-P13) |

## Points in each tree

| Class | Tree 1 | Tree 2 | Tree 3 | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Arms 47 | Fury 53 | Protection 50 | 150 |
| Druid | Balance 46 | Feral Combat 51 | Restoration 51 | 148 |
| Hunter | Beast Mastery 44 | Marksmanship 52 | Survival 52 | 148 |
| Priest | Discipline 51 | Holy 48 | Shadow 49 | 148 |
| Shaman | Elemental 46 | Enhancement 51 | Restoration 51 | 148 |
| Warlock | Affliction 46 | Demonology 50 | Destruction 50 | 146 |
| Mage | Arcane 49 | Fire 46 | Frost 48 | 143 |
| Rogue | Assassination 48 | Combat 44 | Subtlety 47 | 139 |
| **Paladin, beta of 30 October** | Holy 46 | Protection 49 | Retribution 44 | **139** |
| **Paladin, proposed** | Holy 46 | Protection 46 | Retribution 44 | **136** |

- The other classes have 139–150 points (mean 146). The beta Paladin is equal to the Rogue.
- With the proposals, the Paladin has 136 points, the smallest class. Toughness removal (C-P13) deletes 5 ranks, and Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11) adds 2.
- **The small total is not a problem.** The points come from ranks, not talents: each Paladin tree has 17 talents. Smaller trees give more points to the other trees. Rule 1 needs these dips: Holy uses 20 points outside its tree, the tank 17, Retribution 19.
- Do not add ranks to reach the totals of the other classes. New ranks become stat filler, as Toughness was.

**Thin rows**

| Tree | Rows 1–7 | Rows 1–3 | Rows 4–6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Holy | 10 / 12 / 8 / 5 / 5 / 5 / 1 | 30 | **15** |
| Protection | 10 / 7 / 8 / 9 / 8 / 3 / 1 | 25 | 20 |
| Retribution | 10 / 8 / 8 / 6 / 6 / 5 / 1 | 26 | 17 |
| Mean of the other 24 trees | — | 28.6 | 19.1 |

- **Holy rows 4–6 (15 points)** are equal to the lowest other tree (Discipline). A deep Holy build takes all of them except Divine Precision and Consecrated Ground. This is the cause of the 2 points of filler in HB2 (Holy Conduit).
- **Protection and Retribution** are in the range of the other trees.

## Result

- Rules 1 and 2 work for Holy and Protection. Retribution rows 1–2 give the most to the other specs. Retribution row 3 is for Retribution and PvP only.
- Toughness removal (C-P13) gives Holy the Protection investment in row 3.
- The Paladin has the fewest points of all classes (136). This is acceptable, because the trees have the same number of talents as the other classes, and the dips need the free points.
- The earlier balance analysis (calculator version 5, beta of 18 October) is in the history of the repository.
