# Tree balance

**Question.** Do the proposed Paladin trees follow the design rules of Forever, and are they in balance with the other classes?

This tab uses the proposed trees of calculator version 6 (the beta of 30 October 2026, with the proposals). The builds come from Builds. The comparison uses the 24 trees of the other classes (wowsims Forever talent data, 25 October 2026).

## The three rules

1. **Rows 1 and 2 must give something to the other specs.** For example, a Retribution Paladin takes a Holy row 1 talent.
2. **Row 3 is an investment choice.** Another spec puts 10 points or more in the tree for a row 3 talent.
3. **Talents must be in parity with the other classes, not equal.** A Paladin talent can be stronger when this is a strength of the class.

## Short answer

- **Rule 1 works.** Each tree gives something to two specs, except Holy for the tank. The tank already takes 17 points in Retribution.
- **Rule 2 works for Holy and Protection.** Retribution row 3 is only for Retribution and PvP.
- **Rule 3 works.** Most Paladin talents are equal to the talents of other classes. The stronger ones are intentional or Classic values.
- **The Paladin has the fewest points of all classes:** 136 with the proposals. This is not a problem: the trees have as many talents as the other classes. Fewer ranks leave more points for the dips into other trees.

## 1. How the other classes use rows 1–3

| Row | Usual content | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A main stat, or +5% critical hit or hit, for all specs of the class | Cruelty, Malice, Lethal Attacks, Thundering Strikes (+5% critical hit). Elemental Precision, Shadow Focus, Suppression (+5% hit). Ancestral Knowledge, Heart of the Wild (+10% stat). Deflection (parry) |
| 2 | Efficiency, utility, more stats. Sometimes the healer mana talent | Precision (Rogue), Mental Strength, Mindfulness, Nature's Majesty, Elusiveness |
| 3 | A 1-point signature talent, and investment talents | Anger Management, Last Stand, Riposte, Pyroblast, Inner Focus, Holy Nova, Water Shield, Feral Charge. Healer mana: Meditation, Reflection |

## 2. Rule 1: rows 1 and 2 give something to the other specs

| Tree | What Retribution takes | What Protection takes | What Holy takes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Holy | +10% Strength, mana during casts, +15% Seals and Judgements (RB1: 14 points) | Nothing | — |
| Protection | +10% Intellect (RB1: 5 points). PvP: Guardian's Favor, Sacred Duty | — | +10% Intellect, Hand of Protection each 3 min, Divine Shield −60 sec (HB2: 12 points) |
| Retribution | — | Parry, instant spells −10%, Judgement −2 sec, +6% critical hit, Judgement mana (PT1: 17 points) | Instant spells −10%, +6% critical heal chance (HB2: 8 points) |

- **Holy gives nothing to the tank.** The tank already has the most points outside its tree (17, in Retribution).
- **The Holy dip into Protection has no filler.** Divine Intellect opens row 2. Guardian's Favor and Sacred Duty open row 3 for Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11). All of these are saves or mana.

## 3. Rule 2: row 3 is an investment

| Tree | Row 3 talents | Who puts 10 points or more in the tree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Holy | Divine Favor (1 point), Illumination, Holy Conduit | Retribution: Holy Conduit (RB1, 14 Holy points) |
| Protection | Templar's Bulwark (1 point), Improved Hammer of Justice, Shield Specialization, Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11) | Holy: Devoted Sacrifice (12 points). PvP: Hammer of Justice each 45 sec and Swift Judgement (20–25 points) |
| Retribution | Seal of Command (1 point), Vindication, Unyielding Faith, Pursuit of Justice | None. The tank and Holy stop in row 2 |

- **Toughness removal (C-P13) moves Devoted Sacrifice to row 3.** This gives Holy a reason to invest in Protection. In row 4, it cost 17 points, and Holy did not take it.
- **Retribution row 3 has no investment for the other specs.** Unyielding Faith (fear) and Pursuit of Justice (run speed) are PvP talents.

## 4. Rule 3: parity with the other classes

| Paladin talent | Talents of other classes | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Divine Intellect (+10% Intellect, Protection row 1) | Ancestral Knowledge (row 1), Mental Strength (row 2) | Equal |
| Deflection (+5% parry, Retribution row 1) | Deflection (Warrior, Hunter, row 1) | Equal |
| Benediction (instant spells −10%, Retribution row 1) | Convection (−10%, row 1) | Equal |
| Conviction (+6% critical hit, 3 points, Retribution row 2) | Cruelty, Malice, Lethal Attacks (+5%, 5 points, row 1) | Stronger for each point, but one row deeper |
| Precision (+3% hit, Protection row 2) | Precision (Rogue, row 2), Elemental Precision (row 1) | Equal |
| Reverence (15/30%, Holy row 2) | Mindfulness (row 2), Meditation and Reflection (row 3) | Equal row, but weaker: 30% against 50% |
| Improved Seals (+15% Seals and Judgements, Holy row 2) | Concussion (+5%), Improved Eviscerate (+20%) | Parity |
| Improved Judgement (−2 sec, 2 points) | Improved Mind Blast (−2.5 sec, 5 points) | Stronger for each point. It is the Classic value |
| Anticipation (+20 Defense, Protection row 4) | Anticipation (Warrior, row 2) | Weaker: two rows deeper |
| Two-Handed Weapon Specialization (+6% physical, Retribution row 4) | Two-Handed Weapon Specialization (Warrior, +3%, row 4) | Stronger, on purpose: Retribution is the two-hand spec of the class |
| Toughness (removed, C-P13) | No armor talent for the Protection Warrior. Thick Hide (Druid) | The tank with Redoubt (C-P1) does not need it |

## 5. Points in each tree

| Class | Tree 1 | Tree 2 | Tree 3 | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Warrior | Arms 47 | Fury 53 | Protection 50 | 150 |
| Druid | Balance 46 | Feral Combat 51 | Restoration 51 | 148 |
| Hunter | Beast Mastery 44 | Marksmanship 52 | Survival 52 | 148 |
| Priest | Discipline 51 | Holy 48 | Shadow 49 | 148 |
| Shaman | Elemental 46 | Enhancement 51 | Restoration 51 | 148 |
| Warlock | Affliction 46 | Demonology 50 | Destruction 50 | 146 |
| Mage | Arcane 49 | Fire 46 | Frost 48 | 143 |
| Rogue | Assassination 48 | Combat 44 | Subtlety 47 | 139 |
| **Paladin, beta** | Holy 46 | Protection 49 | Retribution 44 | **139** |
| **Paladin, proposed** | Holy 46 | Protection 46 | Retribution 44 | **136** |

- **The other classes have 139–150 points** (mean 146). The beta Paladin is equal to the Rogue.
- **With the proposals, the Paladin has 136 points.** Toughness removal (C-P13) deletes 5 ranks, and Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11) adds 2.
- **The small total is not a problem.** Each Paladin tree has 17 talents, as the other classes. Fewer ranks leave more points for dips: Holy takes 20 points outside its tree, the tank 17 and Retribution 19.
- **Do not add ranks to reach the other totals.** New ranks become stat filler, as Toughness was.

**Points in each row**

| Tree | Rows 1–7 | Rows 1–3 | Rows 4–6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Holy | 10 / 12 / 8 / 5 / 5 / 5 / 1 | 30 | **15** |
| Protection | 10 / 7 / 8 / 9 / 8 / 3 / 1 | 25 | 20 |
| Retribution | 10 / 8 / 8 / 6 / 6 / 5 / 1 | 26 | 17 |
| Mean of the other 24 trees | — | 28.6 | 19.1 |

- **Holy rows 4–6 have only 15 points,** equal to the lowest other tree (Discipline). A deep Holy build takes almost all of them. This causes the 2 filler points of HB2 (Holy Conduit).
- **Protection and Retribution** are in the range of the other trees.

## 6. Limits

- The comparison counts talents and points. It does not measure the value of each talent in a fight.
- The talent data of the other classes is from 25 October 2026. A later beta can change it.
- The earlier balance analysis (calculator version 5, beta of 18 October) is in the history of the repository.
