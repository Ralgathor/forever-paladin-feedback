# Healing

**Question.** How well does the Holy Paladin heal against the other healers of Forever: Priest, Druid and Shaman?

The design goal of Holy is a healer for long fights and the best healer on one target. It does not need to be the best raid healer. One Holy talent proposal stays: Lingering Light (C-H2). The other values are for the live Holy Paladin.

All values are healing each sec after overhealing, at 715 healing power, from the healing model. A critical heal heals for 200% (Holy Power 5/5, main hypothesis).

## Short answer

- **On one tank, the Paladin and the Druid are about equal.** The Paladin is 2% below to 7% above the Druid. In fights of 10 min, the Druid is first. The Paladin is 14–21% above the Shaman and the Priest.
- **After a large hit on the tank, the Paladin is first with all cooldowns:** 6% above the Druid in the first 3 sec. With short cooldowns only, it is 6–7% below the Druid.
- **In raids, the Paladin is the weakest healer:** 22–30% below the Druid, 13–17% below the Shaman and 37–44% below the Priest. This is accepted, because Holy is not a raid healer first.
- **Why the Paladin is weak in raids.** Oath of Light helps only one ally. The other healers have strong cooldown heals for the raid: Prayer of Mending, Riptide, Swiftmend and Tranquility.
- **The Paladin has the least mana.** Reverence keeps only 30% of the Spirit regeneration during casts. The other healers keep 50%.
- **The one-tank result uses the 200% hypothesis.** Holy Power can change it. At 225%, the Paladin is 3–10% above the Druid. At 175%, it is 1–7% below. A live test must give the real value (Open items).
- **Lingering Light (C-H2) is a proposal again (2 November).** On one tank, it gives +4–11% against the Druid at 200%, thus a first place in all fight lengths. At 225%, it gives +10–18%, below the 20% limit. Reverence 50% (C-H1) stays withdrawn.

- **The Paladin is last in raids.**
- **It is 2–5% lower than in the beta of 18 October.** Holy Power gives larger critical heals, but Blessing of Light is gone.
- **The Paladin casts mostly Flash of Light,** and Divine Favor with Holy Light each 2 min. In fights of 3 min, it also casts about 5 Light's Vigil. Its mana is empty before the end of fights of 3 and 5 min.
- **Oath of Light is not a raid tool.** With Oath of Light on all heals (not possible in the game), the Paladin is only equal to the Shaman.

## 3. Recovery after a large hit

The healing on the tank in the first 3 and 6 sec after a large hit, with no overhealing. "Long cooldowns" are Divine Favor, Nature's Swiftness and Inner Focus.

Before the hit, each healer heals the tank as usual. Thus, its heals over time are already on the tank at the hit: Renew, Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Riptide and Healing Stream Totem.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Druid | 6,293 | 10,041 | 4,898 | 8,686 | 6,839 | 10,860 |
| Shaman | 4,750 | 8,054 | 3,361 | 6,664 | 5,119 | 8,689 |
| Paladin, beta of 18 October | 5,024 | 8,232 | 3,947 | 7,155 | 5,337 | 8,745 |

- **With all cooldowns, the Paladin is first:** 6% above the Druid after 3 sec, 2% after 6 sec. Divine Favor and Holy Light with Oath of Light heal for about 5,600.
- **With short cooldowns only, the Druid is first:** the Paladin is 6–7% below. The Druid heals over time are already on the tank, and Swiftmend heals for a full Rejuvenation.
- **The Paladin is 27% or more above the Shaman and the Priest** after 6 sec, with all cooldowns.
- **At 225%,** the Paladin is 18% above the Druid after 3 sec. With short cooldowns only, it is 2% below.
- Power Word: Shield is not in the table. It absorbs about 1,100 damage.

## 6. Holy Power: 225%, 200% or 175%

The Holy Power text says: "increases the critical heal bonus of Holy spells by 50%". A normal critical heal heals for 150%. The text has three possible meanings:

- **200% (main hypothesis):** +50 points on 150%.
- **225% (wowsims and the server rule):** ×1.5 on the full critical heal.
- **175% (the Classic rule of Ice Shards and Ruin):** +50% of the bonus.

| Case | Raid against the Druid, 3 / 5 / 10 min | One tank against the Druid, 3 / 5 / 10 min |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 225% | −26% / −23% / −26% | +8% / +10% / +3% |
| **200% (main)** | **−30% / −27% / −29%** | **+2% / +5% / −2%** |
| 175% | −34% / −31% / −33% | −3% / −1% / −7% |

- **At 225%,** the Paladin is the best healer on one tank in all fight lengths.
- **At 200%,** it is first only in fights of 3 and 5 min.
- **At 175%,** the Druid is first in fights of 3 and 10 min. The design goal is not reached.
- **The test (Open items):** take Holy Power 5/5, cast Divine Favor, then Holy Light on a target with no Oath of Light. Compare the heal with the tooltip average: 2 times is 200%, 2.25 times is 225%, 1.75 times is 175%.

## 7. Options examined

Lingering Light (C-H2) is a proposal. The other options are withdrawn (see Withdrawn ideas). The values at 200% are here for a decision after the Holy Power test.

| Option | Raid against the Druid, 3 / 5 / 10 min | One tank against the Druid, 3 / 5 / 10 min |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live version | −30% / −27% / −29% | +2% / +5% / −2% |
| **Lingering Light 2/2 (C-H2, proposal)** | **−26% / −22% / −25%** | **+8% / +11% / +4%** |
| Reverence 50% (C-H1) | −26% / −22% / −23% | +7% / +11% / +6% |
| Reverence 50% and Lingering Light 2/2 | −22% / −17% / −18% | +14% / +18% / +13% |
| Boundless Light also +15% critical chance for Flash of Light | −21% / −15% / −16% | +2% / +5% / −2% |
| Flash of Light +30% critical chance | −13% / −3% / 0% | +2% / +5% / −2% |

- **C-H1 and C-H2 together** give 13–18% above the Druid on one tank, below the 20% limit. At 225%, they gave 20–25%, past the limit. This was the reason to withdraw them on 31 October.
- **Lingering Light alone** gives +15% / +18% / +10% at 225% and +2% / +5% / −2% at 175%. It stays below the limit in all three cases. Thus, it is a proposal again.
- **Boundless Light for Flash of Light** changes only the raid values, because the tank has Oath of Light.
- **Infusion of Light on all hits (C-H3)** adds nothing in the model, because the Paladin casts almost only Flash of Light.

## 8. Inputs of the model

**Gear and buffs**

- 715 healing power, 315 Intellect, 249 Spirit, 40 MP5, 4% spell critical chance. Parts 1 and 2 also give 900 healing power.
- Raid buffs give stats, mana and +3% spell critical chance. Each healer has 117 MP5.
- One major mana potion each 2 min (1,800 mana) and one Mana Tide Totem each 5 min (1,160 mana).
- Mana pool: Classic base mana plus 15 mana for each Intellect point. Spirit regeneration: the Classic 1.12 formula of each class.

**Paladin build (38/5/8)**

- Holy: Healing Light 3/3 (+12%), Reverence 2/2, Illumination 5/5, Divine Favor, Holy Shock, Holy Power 5/5, Boundless Light 2/2, Light's Vigil, Purifying Power 1.
- Divine Intellect 5/5, Benediction 5/5, Conviction 3/3 (+6% critical chance). Base spell critical chance 3.5% (game files).
- Oath of Light on one ally: Holy Light +400, Flash of Light +115, Holy Shock +229.
- Boundless Light 2/2: Holy Shock +30% critical chance on an ally with no Oath of Light.
- Light's Vigil rank 3: 1,340 mana. The next Holy Shock heals each party member for 684–724.

**Other healers**

- Druid: Gift of Nature, Improved Rejuvenation, Genesis, Improved Regrowth, Gift of the Earthmother, Nature's Splendor.
- Priest: Prayer of Mending and Binding Heal, no Penance.
- Shaman: Purification, Healing Way, Tidal Mastery.

**Rules**

- **Cast plan.** A linear program finds the best mix of casts for the mana and the fight length.
- **Critical ticks.** In Forever, each tick of a heal over time can be a critical heal. The model gives each tick the critical chance of the class.
- **Raid and one tank.** "Raid" counts all heals. "One tank" counts only the heals on the tank.
- **Overhealing.** Flash of Light 15%, Holy Light 20%, Holy Shock 15%, Light's Vigil 25%, Prayer of Mending 15%, Renew, Greater Heal and Binding Heal 25%, Regrowth and Rejuvenation 25%, Swiftmend 20%, Tranquility 40%, Wild Growth 50%, Riptide and Chain Heal 25%, Healing Wave 20%, Healing Stream Totem 50%.
- **Not in the model.** Power Word: Shield, Divine Aegis, Inspiration, Ancestral Healing, Lay on Hands and Nature's Swiftness.

## 9. Method and limits

- **Source.** The model uses the method of the DoubleZug sheets for the Forever healers. Three reviews corrected it (22 October 2026). A check against the game files in wowsims Forever (23 October 2026) corrected the Paladin critical chance, Regrowth and Chain Heal.
- **Scripts.** `models/healing/healing_model.py` (Priest, Druid, Shaman, solver), `models/beta-30-oct/healing_b2.py` (Paladin), `recovery_b2.py` (part 3), `holy_proposals_b2.py` (part 7).
- **Holy Power** is the largest open question (part 6).
- **Prayer of Mending.** The model gives it 5 targets. With 3 targets, the raid Priest gives 786, not 948.
- **Omen of Clarity.** With 2 free Wild Growth casts each min, the raid Druid gives 940, not 855.
- **Mana Tide Totem.** With two totems, the raid Shaman gives 742, not 709.
- **Swiftmend.** A player test shows that it does not remove the heal over time. If it does, the Druid gives 833 in raids and 616 on one tank.
- **Nature's Splendor** needs 11 Balance points. Without it, the Druid gives 747 in raids and 626 on one tank.
- **Classic tick rule.** With no critical ticks, the Paladin is 12–14% below the Shaman and 23–27% below the Druid in raids. On one tank, it is 1–8% above the Druid.
- **Overhealing** values are estimates. They change with the raid and the healer tasks.
- **Oath of Light.** The model puts all tank heals on the Oath target. With two tanks, only one has the Oath.
- **Light's Vigil.** The model gives the Paladin a full party of 5.
- **No wowsims check.** wowsims Forever has no Holy heal rotation and no Druid or Shaman heals. The earlier check (beta of 18 October) found the Paladin model within −4.3% to +5.3% of wowsims.
