# Holy

The beta of 30 October 2026 changes most of the Holy tree. Oath of Light, Boundless Light and the new Holy Power make the Paladin the best healer on one tank. This is the design goal of Holy. In raids, the Paladin stays below the other healers (see Beta test results). This is accepted, because Holy is not a raid healer first. Thus, no Holy talent proposal stays. The spell changes (C1, C2, C4, C5) still apply to Holy.

## Holy layout

| Row (points necessary) | Talents | Points in the row |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (0) | Spiritual Focus, Divine Strength | 10 |
| 2 (5) | Healing Light, Reverence, Improved Seals, Purifying Power | 12 |
| 3 (10) | Divine Favor, Illumination, Holy Conduit | 8 |
| 4 (15) | Divine Precision, Infusion of Light, Oath of Light | 5 |
| 5 (20) | Consecrated Ground, Holy Shock, Boundless Light | 5 |
| 6 (25) | Holy Power | 5 |
| 7 (30) | Light's Vigil | 1 |

- Holy has 46 points, the same as the beta.
- All talents keep their beta places and values.
- Infusion of Light keeps its beta text. The proposal for no critical hit and a Holy Strike trigger (C-H3) is withdrawn (see Withdrawn ideas).
- Reverence keeps 15/30%. The proposal for 25/50% (C-H1) is withdrawn (see Withdrawn ideas).
- Healing Light and Oath of Light keep their beta values. The proposal for Healing Light 10/20/30% (C-H4) is withdrawn (see Withdrawn ideas).
