# Protection

The Protection proposals give:

- A fix for crushing blows (Redoubt, C-P1). This is the largest difference between the Paladin and the Warrior.
- A simpler tree (Iron Creed, C-P2; Toughness and Divine Strength removal, C-P3; Improved Righteous Fury removal, C-P4; Shoulder the Burden, C-P5).
- Rows 1 and 2 with a use for each point (Toughness and Divine Strength removal, C-P3; Benediction, M-P1).
- A Reckoning that works at level 60 (C-P6).
- A ranged pull (Reach of Law, C-P7).
- More stable mana for tanks (Shield Specialization, C-P8).
- Threat that increases with gear (Holy Shield, C-P9).
- A Lay on Hands that does not drain all the mana, and a Hand of Sacrifice that costs less health (Devoted Sacrifice, C-P11).

## C-P1. Redoubt: Holy Shield also starts it

- **Proposed text.** "Damaging melee attacks against you have a 10% chance to increase your chance to block by 4/8/12/16/20%. Lasts 10 sec or 5 blocks. Casting Holy Shield also triggers this effect."
- **Change.** Only the Holy Shield trigger is new. If Redoubt is active, a new trigger sets it again to 10 sec and 5 blocks.
- **Why.** It removes most crushing blows (see Analysis, Crushing blows).
- **Blizzard's direction.** The patch of 1 October moved block from Redoubt (6–30% → 4–20%) to Holy Shield (20% → 30%). Redoubt (C-P1) keeps these values. Redoubt is only active for a longer time.
- **Other specs.** Holy and Retribution do not have Holy Shield. Thus, their Redoubt does not change.
- **Side effects.** Block from talents increases from about 40% (30% Holy Shield + about 10% Redoubt) to 50%. Reckoning, Shield Specialization and Holy Shield receive a little more value.

## C-P2. Iron Creed: damage reduction while Righteous Fury is active

- **Proposed text.** "Increases the threat generated by your Holy Strike ability by 5/10/15/20/25%. While Righteous Fury is active, reduces your damage taken by 2/4/6/8/10%."
- **Problem.** In the beta, the reduction lasts 6 sec after a Holy Strike. Holy Strike has a 10 sec cooldown. At 5/5, the average is 6 sec ÷ 10 sec × 10% = 6%.
- **Change.** The reduction is active while Righteous Fury is active. It has no timer and no Holy Strike condition. Thus, Holy Strike has only one more mechanic (Reckoning, C-P6).
- **Threat bonus.** The +25% Holy Strike threat stays. It gives about 10 threat per second (TPS) at 5/5 and 200 spell power (see Analysis, Threat).
- **Total.** −10% damage taken. This is the same as Defensive Stance (Warrior).
- **Rule exception.** Iron Creed works as an improved Righteous Fury. But the full 10% needs 30 Protection points. Monitor it in PvP, because all specs can use Righteous Fury.

## C-P3. Toughness and Divine Strength: removed, Vindication with +2/4/6% Attack Power

- **Change.** Toughness (Protection row 1) and Divine Strength (Holy row 1) leave the trees. Three talents move to fill rows 1 and 2. Benediction (M-P1) and Unyielding Faith (M-H2) only move. Vindication also gives more Attack Power (C-P3):

| Talent | Beta | Proposed | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Benediction (M-P1) | Retribution row 1, column 3 | Protection row 1, column 2 | The same: 5 ranks, 2/4/6/8/10% |
| Vindication (C-P3) | Retribution row 3, column 1 | Retribution row 2, column 3 | 3 ranks, +2/4/6% Attack Power (see below) |
| Unyielding Faith (M-H2) | Holy row 2, column 4 | Holy row 1, column 2 | The same |

- **The other talents stay in their beta place.** Precision, Guardian's Favor and Anticipation stay in Protection row 2. Holy Conduit stays in Retribution row 2. Only Conviction moves in Retribution (M-R2).
- **Benediction text.** "Reduces the Mana cost of all instant cast spells and abilities by 2/4/6/8/10%." The same as the beta.
- **Vindication text.** "Gives your damaging melee attacks a chance to reduce the target's Attack Power by 67/136/204, and increase your Attack Power by 2/4/6% for 30 sec." Beta: +1/2/3%. The debuff on the target does not change.
- **Why remove Toughness.** Forever removed flat armor talents for tanks. Toughness (Warrior) was removed. Toughness (Shaman) now gives Stamina. Thick Hide (Druid) increases with Defense. Toughness (Paladin) was the last flat armor talent.
- **Why remove Divine Strength.** +10% Strength gives a tank about +15 Strength with pre-raid gear. This is less than 1 threat per sec for each point. Retribution receives about +7 DPS for 5 points, the least of its talents (see Analysis, Builds).
- **Goal.** Each point in rows 1 and 2 has a use for its spec. The other specs keep a reason to put points in each tree (design rule 2). The other talents stay near the beta tree.
- **Why Benediction goes to Protection (M-P1).** It takes the place of Toughness. Its main users are Holy and Retribution: Benediction 5 opens Protection row 2 for them. Tank builds take the Retribution dip first, because Benediction gives no threat (see Analysis, Builds).
- **Protection.** A tank uses Redoubt, Precision (threat), Anticipation (Defense against bosses) and Guardian's Favor (saves). Benediction is the mana choice. At 5/5, it saves about 4 mana per sec without Consecration and 11 with Consecration (see Analysis, Mana).
- **Retribution.** Benediction 5 opens Protection row 2. Retribution then takes Precision 3 (+3% hit). This dip is 8 points. Benediction also decreases the cost of Judgement, Holy Strike, Seals and Consecration.
- **Holy.** Benediction 5 opens Protection row 2. Holy then takes Guardian's Favor 2. Holy Shock and Cleanse cost 10% less. This dip is 7 points.
- **Holy Conduit.** It stays in Retribution row 2, as in the beta. A tank takes it with Deflection 5 and Holy Conduit 2 (7 points). Deflection is also a tank talent. With Benediction and Holy Conduit, Consecration on cooldown costs 76 mana per sec, not 112 (see Analysis, Mana).
- **Why Vindication goes to row 2.** It takes the row 2 place of Conviction (M-R2). +2% Attack Power for each point gives each point the value of a 5-rank talent point. +6% Attack Power gives about +7 DPS.
- **Why Unyielding Faith goes to row 1 (M-H2).** Improved Seals (M-H1) keeps its 3 beta ranks. Unyielding Faith fills the other 2 points of Holy row 1. Retribution and Protection open Holy row 2 with Improved Seals 3 and Unyielding Faith 2. Retribution then takes Infusion of Light. Iron Will (Warrior) is also in row 1.
- **Shape.** Each row 1 has 10 points. Holy uses 5 + 3 + 2, as Fire (Mage). Protection and Retribution use 5 + 5. Holy has 43 points, Protection 49 and Retribution 39 (44 with Burning Zeal and Measure for Measure).
- **Parity.** Convection (Shaman) gives −10% mana cost for 5 points in Elemental row 1. Benediction gives the same in Protection row 1.

- Iron Creed alone gives 10%, the same as the Warrior.

## C-P5. Shoulder the Burden (new talent) replaces Improved Seal of Fury: row 3, column 1

- **Proposed text.** "You shoulder the burden of a friendly party or raid member, transferring 50% of their threat against all enemies to you." Instant, 30 yards, 3 min cooldown, 45 mana (3% of base mana).
- **Why remove Improved Seal of Fury.** It was a second "mana when you are hit" talent. Its mana return does not change with the rank of the Seal. Sanctified Judgement (Retribution row 3) is the other mana talent for a tank now (see Mana).
- **Problem.** The Paladin tank has no area taunt and no threat transfer. Judgement of Fury taunts only one enemy, at 10 yards. Thus, tank swaps and threat on a group of enemies are difficult.
- **Change.** Shoulder the Burden moves half of the threat of the ally to the Paladin, on each enemy. The ally keeps the other half. Thus, the ally must still control their threat.
- **Model.** Fade (Priest) and Feint (Rogue) decrease the threat of a player. Shoulder the Burden also moves that threat to the tank.
- **Raw threat.** The threat that moves does not change. Righteous Fury does not multiply it.
- **Tank swaps.** First use Judgement of Fury to take the boss. Then use Shoulder the Burden on the other tank. You then have 1.5 times their threat, and they keep 0.5 times. Do not use it before the taunt: the threat of the other tank decreases, and a damage dealer can then pull the boss.
- **PvP.** Players do not use threat. Thus, the talent has no effect in PvP.
- **Why 3 min.** It works on all enemies that the ally has threat on, at any range, and the threat stays moved. Thus, it is near an area taunt. Challenging Shout (Warrior) has 10 min. Mocking Blow (Warrior) has 2 min, but it works on one target for 6 sec. At 5 min, a dungeon tank uses it on about one pull in five, and a raid tank one time for each boss. At 3 min, a raid tank uses it 2–3 times in each boss fight.
- **Sacred Duty.** Sacred Duty does not decrease the cooldown of Shoulder the Burden. The 3 min cooldown is the final value. Sacred Duty keeps its beta text.
- **Position.** Row 3, column 1, the beta place of Improved Seal of Fury.
- **Swift Judgement.** In the beta, it requires Improved Seal of Fury. Shoulder the Burden (C-P5) removes Improved Seal of Fury. Thus, Swift Judgement stays in row 4 with no requirement.
- **Signature talents.** Each tree keeps a 1-point signature talent in rows 3 and 4:
  - Holy: Voice of Truth and Divine Favor.
  - Retribution: Seal of Command and Sacred Arbiter.
  - Protection: Shoulder the Burden and Swift Judgement.
- **Other specs.** Holy and Retribution can take it with 10 points, but they have no use for it. The threat goes to them, not to a tank. Hand of Protection and Hammer of Justice save an ally without this threat. Thus, only Protection takes Shoulder the Burden.
- **No change.** Righteous Fury, Seal of Fury and its absorb shield stay baseline spells.

## C-P6. Reckoning: charges from block, dodge and parry, released by Holy Strike

- **Proposed text.** "Gives you a 20/40/60/80/100% chance to gain an extra attack after Blocking, Dodging, Parrying or being the victim of a non-periodic critical strike. Cannot occur from Blocks, Dodges or Parries more than once every 4 sec. Stores up to 4 extra attacks. Your Holy Strike releases up to 2 stored extra attacks."
- **Beta text.** "Gives you a 8/16/24/32/40% chance after Blocking and a 20/40/60/80/100% chance after receiving a non-periodic critical strike to gain an extra attack. Stores up to 4 extra attacks. Cannot occur from Blocks more than once every 1.5 sec."
- **Text model.** The extra-attack effects of Classic: Reckoning, Sword Specialization and Hand of Justice.

**Problems in the beta**

- **The critical hit trigger does not work at level 60.** At 440 Defense, a raid boss cannot make critical hits (see Analysis, Crushing blows). Thus, half of the talent works only while you level or in PvP.
- **The storage does not work when you tank.** A tank attacks all the time. Thus, a stored attack hits with the next swing. The storage is useful only when you stop your attacks (the Classic "Reckoning bomb").
- **Low value on a boss:** about one extra attack each 10 sec.

**Changes**

- **New triggers: block, dodge and parry.** They work at the critical hit cap, as Master of Defense (Warrior) does with dodge and parry. At the stat targets, about 86% of the boss swings are blocked, dodged or parried.
- **The ranks increase the chance (20–100%).** The 4 sec limit does not change with the ranks, as with other proc talents.
- **Holy Strike releases the charges, 2 at a time.** One Holy Strike gives a maximum of 3 hits. The charges stay between Holy Strikes. You can keep them for a target change, a taunt or a dangerous enemy.
- **The critical hit trigger stays, without the 4 sec limit.** Thus, the Classic Reckoning bomb still works while you level and in PvP. 4 critical hits taken give 4 charges. Two Holy Strikes release them.
- **A link between the shield and the attacks,** as Shield Slam (Warrior) has. Players on Wowhead say that the Paladin does not have this link.

**Value for a tank at 5/5**

Simulation: the boss swings each 2 sec. 60% block with Redoubt (C-P1), 10.7% dodge, 15% parry. No critical hits. Holy Strike each 10 sec. With Reckoning (C-P6), each extra attack does about 165 damage and about 240 threat (threat model). In the beta, the extra attacks can be glancing blows: about 140 damage and 210 threat. The beta values use 50% block.

| Case | Charges each 10 sec | Extra attacks each 10 sec | Extra damage per sec | Extra TPS |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beta, 1 boss | 1.0 | 1.0 | +14 | +21 |
| Beta, 3 enemies | 2.2 | 2.2 | +30 | +46 |
| **Proposed, 1 boss** | **2.3** | **2.0** | **+33** | **+48** |
| **Proposed, 3 enemies** | **2.4** | **2.0** | **+33** | **+48** |

- **On a boss,** the proposal gives two times the value of the beta: +27 TPS more.
- **The charges come a little faster than Holy Strike uses them** (2.3 and 2.0 each 10 sec). The Paladin usually has 2–4 charges. About 13% of the procs are lost at the maximum of 4.
- **On groups of enemies,** the 4 sec limit keeps the same value as on a boss. The beta gives more attacks on groups (2.2 and 2.0 each 10 sec), because its block trigger has a 1.5 sec limit. But its attacks can be glancing blows. Thus, the damage is almost the same (+30 and +33 per sec).
- **PvP burst.** See Analysis, PvP burst check.

## C-P7. Reach of Law (new talent): a ranged pull for Protection

- **Proposed text.** "Increases the range of your Judgement by 5/10 yards while Seal of Fury is active."
- **Position.** Row 5, column 1, 2 ranks. It requires Swift Judgement (straight arrow from row 4).
- **Why.** Protection has no ranged pull and no ranged taunt. The Seal of Fury Judgement does 153–167 Holy damage and taunts for 4 sec. But its range is only 10 yards. At 2/2, the range is 20 yards: two times the base range.
- **Model.** Vanguard (Warrior): Charge in Defensive Stance, row 4, 1 point. Feral Charge (Druid, row 3) does the same for Feral.
- **Why 2 ranks.** Row 5 already has a 1-point talent (Templar's Bulwark). Thus, this talent has ranks.
- **Why only with Seal of Fury.** Judgement of Command does 388–422 damage to a stunned target. Without the Seal of Fury condition, a 20 yard range gives more burst to hybrids in PvP. With the condition, Holy and Retribution receive almost nothing from the dip.
- **Threat and mana.** No change while you tank. Judgement still costs 91 mana and has a 10 sec cooldown. Only the first pull changes. The full path costs 3 points (Swift Judgement and 2 ranks).

## C-P8. Shield Specialization: mana return on each block

- **Proposed text (rank 3).** "Increases the amount of damage absorbed by your shield by 30%, and your blocks restore 6% of your maximum Mana. May only occur once every 2 sec." Ranks: 2/4/6%.
- **Change.** The return occurs on each block at all ranks: 2/4/6% of maximum mana (beta: a 33/66/100% chance to restore 4%). The limit is once each 2 sec (beta: 3 sec). The absorb (10/20/30%) does not change.
- **Why.** Shoulder the Burden (C-P5) removes Improved Seal of Fury (about 23 mana per sec while you tank). Sanctified Judgement 3/3 returns about 17 for the tank builds PB1–PB4. Holy Shield rank 3 costs 24 mana per sec. Without Shield Specialization (C-P8), a fresh level 60 PB1 tank (2,897 mana) loses about 7 mana per sec without Consecration.
- **Value.** A boss that swings each 2 sec can trigger it with each block: 6% of maximum mana × 60% block ÷ 2 sec. This is 52 mana per sec at fresh level 60 and 88 in Naxxramas (beta: 19 and 33). Without Consecration, PB1 receives +26 to +64 mana per sec while it tanks (see Analysis, Mana).
- **Model.** Warriors and Druids receive rage when they are hit. Water Shield (Shaman) gives mana when the Shaman is hit. Shield Specialization is already the Paladin version. Thus, no new talent is necessary.
- **Why 6% and 2 sec.** Improved Seal of Fury gave 23.3 mana per sec at all gear levels. With 6%, PB1 has more mana than in the beta at fresh level 60 (+26 against +16). Ranks increase in equal steps. A 3 sec limit skips the next boss swing: the tank then has less mana than with 4% each 2 sec.
- **Side effect.** From Molten Core, PB1 can use Consecration on cooldown with no mana loss. Consecration then adds threat (W10).
- **Synergy.** More block gives more mana. Thus, the crushing blow fix (Redoubt, C-P1) and the mana fix help each other.
- **Limits.** Against groups of enemies, it gives a maximum of 3% of maximum mana each sec: 87 at fresh level 60, 147 in Naxxramas (beta: 39 and 65). It gives nothing to a Paladin who is not hit. Sanctified Judgement is for that case.

## C-P9. Holy Shield: damage increases with block value

- **Proposed text (rank 3).** "Increases chance to block by 30% for 10 sec, and deals 221 Holy damage, increased by your Block Value, for each attack blocked while active. Damage caused by Holy Shield causes 20% additional threat. Each block expends a charge. 4 charges."
- **Change.** Each block adds 100% of your block value to the Holy Shield damage. The text uses the words of Shield Slam (Warrior): "increased by your Block Value". The block chance, the charges, the cooldown and the threat bonus do not change.
- **Problem.** Warrior and Druid threat increases with gear and with boss damage, because their rage comes from the hits they take. Paladin threat increases only with spell power: about +33 TPS for each +100 spell power. Without Holy Shield (C-P9), the Paladin has 95% of the Druid threat at fresh level 60, but 75% in Naxxramas (see Analysis, Threat).
- **Why block value.** It is a gear stat on the character sheet. It is easy to read and easy to collect. It increases from 26 at fresh level 60 to 108 at pre-raid and about 230 in Naxxramas, much faster than spell power. It connects the tank stats of the Paladin: the shield, Strength and Shield Specialization (+30% block value). Shield Slam (Warrior) already adds 100% of block value to its damage.
- **Result.** The Paladin has 99% of the Druid threat at fresh level 60 and 94% in Naxxramas. With flatter gear progression, it has 91–95% in Naxxramas (see Threat, part 3). In wowsims, the PT3 tank with the pre-raid gear of builds/protection.md has 116% of the Warrior threat (see Threat, part 7).
- **Why 100%.** It is the value of Shield Slam. Forever pre-raid gear already gives 108 block value (Wowhead Forever values). At 200%, wowsims gives PT3 123% of the Warrior threat at pre-raid, and the model gives 113% of the Druid threat in Naxxramas. The Warrior and Druid values have some unknown threat values (see Threat, part 6).
- **No new button.** You already use Holy Shield on cooldown. Redoubt (C-P1) keeps about 3 blocks each 10 sec.
- **PvP.** At about 108 block value, each block does about 108 more Holy damage. The maximum is 4 blocks each 10 sec, against melee attacks only. Only Protection has Holy Shield.

## C-P11. Devoted Sacrifice (new talent): row 3, column 2

- **Proposed text.** "Your Lay on Hands drains only 75/50% of your remaining Mana, and reduces the damage you take from your Hand of Sacrifice by 20/40%." 2 ranks.
- **Position.** Row 3, column 2, the beta place of Improved Righteous Fury. Improved Righteous Fury removal (C-P4) frees this place.
- **Theme.** The Paladin gives its own resources to save others. Lay on Hands costs mana, and Hand of Sacrifice costs health. Devoted Sacrifice makes the two spells cost less.
- **Lay on Hands.** The spell keeps its heal, its 20 min cooldown and its mana for the target.
- **Problem.** Lay on Hands drains all the mana of the Paladin (Forever spellbook, all ranks). Forever decreases the cooldown from 1 hour to 20 min. Thus, the Paladin can use it about one time for each boss. But the drain is largest when the mana is full, early in a fight. Thus, Paladins use it only when their mana is already low.
- **Keep mana for the role.** With Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11), the Paladin can use Lay on Hands at any time and always keeps half of the mana. The healer continues to heal, the tank keeps its threat, and Retribution keeps its damage. The time until the mana is back is also half (see the table).
- **Other saves.** At 0 mana, the Paladin cannot cast Divine Shield (110 mana), Hand of Protection (7% of base mana) or Hammer of Justice (100 mana). With Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11), these saves stay available.
- **Hand of Sacrifice.** The pool does not change. The target is still protected from up to 825 damage (Hand of Sacrifice, C4). Only the damage that the Paladin takes decreases: about 660 at 1/2 and 495 at 2/2. A tank takes the damage of an ally in addition to the boss damage. Thus, without Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11), a tank uses Hand of Sacrifice only when its health is high.
- **Why row 3.** Lay on Hands comes at level 10. Thus, the talent has a use when row 3 opens (level 20). The Hand of Sacrifice part has a use from level 46. Improved Reincarnation (Shaman, Restoration row 2) is the same: it gives +2% health from level 15, and Reincarnation comes at level 30.
- **Why 20/40%.** At rank 1, the drain is 75%. Lay on Hands is then more efficient than the usual heals only below 21% mana. Thus, the Hand of Sacrifice part gives the value of rank 1. At 40%, the Paladin still takes more than half of the damage. With Hand of Sacrifice each 15 sec (C4), 2/2 removes about 22 damage per sec from the raid.
- **Model.** Improved Health Funnel (Warlock) and Improved Mend Pet (Hunter) are 2-rank talents for save spells. Sacred Duty also gives two effects in one talent.
- **Cost for Holy.** Holy takes it with Benediction 5, Guardian's Favor 2 and Redoubt 3: 12 Protection points.
- **Builds.** HB1, HB2, HB3 and RB3 take it (see Analysis, Builds).
- **PvP.** Divine Shield already stops all the damage that moves (W20). Thus, the Hand of Sacrifice part does not change the Divine Shield risk.
- **Design rule 1.** Lay on Hands and Hand of Sacrifice are not buffs (Hands, C5). Thus, Devoted Sacrifice (C-P11) does not break the rule.
- **Tree size.** Protection goes from 47 to 49 points. The Warrior Protection tree has 50 (see Analysis, Tree balance).

**Cast at full mana**

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Holy (4,416, +22.6 per sec) | About 4,000 | 3 min 15 sec | 2,208: 1 min 38 sec |
| Protection PB1, fresh level 60 (2,897, +25.7 per sec while it tanks) | About 5,500 | 1 min 52 sec | 1,449: 56 sec |

The mana values use a fresh level 60 Holy profile with no raid buffs and no consumables: 4,416 mana and 22.6 mana per sec.

**Why 50%**

At 900 healing power and 19% critical heals, Flash of Light and Holy Light give 5.75 healing for each mana point. This value includes Blessing of Light removal (C3). Thus, 4,000 healing costs about 695 mana. If the drain is less than 695 mana, Lay on Hands is more efficient than the usual heals.

| Drain (% of the current mana) | Drain at full mana (Holy) | More efficient than the usual heals below | Mana that stays after a cast at 30% mana |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25% | 1,104 | 63% mana | 994 |
| 40% | 1,766 | 39% mana | 795 |
| 50% (Devoted Sacrifice, C-P11, rank 2) | 2,208 | 31% mana | 662 |
| 75% (Devoted Sacrifice, C-P11, rank 1) | 3,312 | 21% mana | 331 |
| 100% (beta) | 4,416 | 16% mana | 0 |

- Below 50%, a healer can use Lay on Hands for mana at half mana. Then it is not an emergency heal.
- Above 60%, the drain is almost the same as the beta.
- With better gear, the healing for each mana point increases more than the health. Thus, 50% becomes safer in late raids.

**Mana for the target**

- Rank 3 restores 550 mana to the target. The drain occurs first.
- If the Paladin casts it on itself, the Paladin receives more mana than it loses below 1,100 mana (beta: below 550). The maximum is +550 in the two cases, at 0 mana.
- This is about one time for each boss: about 5% of the mana of a Holy Paladin in a 5 min fight. A Major Mana Potion gives 1,350–2,250 each 2 min. See W18.

## M-P1. Benediction: Retribution row 1 → Protection row 1

- **Text.** "Reduces the Mana cost of all instant cast spells and abilities by 2/4/6/8/10%." 5 ranks, the same as the beta.
- **Position.** Protection row 1, column 2. It takes the place of Toughness (C-P3).
- **Why.** Holy and Retribution open Protection row 2 with it. Tank builds take the Retribution dip first, because Benediction gives no threat. See Toughness and Divine Strength removal (C-P3) for the full reason.
